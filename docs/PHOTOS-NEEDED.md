# Photos to re-upload in full quality

These 106 photos are on the site at only 800 pixels wide. That is why the middle of the story (Aug 2024 to Apr 2026) looks soft and blurry, especially on a big screen. They are the original photos from the first version of the site, and no bigger copies exist in Drive or GitHub.

**What to do**

1. Find each photo in Google Photos or your phone gallery. The file name is the date and time it was taken, e.g. `20240826_182222.jpg` = Aug 26, 2024 at 6:22:22 pm. Searching the date in Google Photos finds them fastest.
2. Download the **original** (not a screenshot, not a WhatsApp copy).
3. If a photo is bigger than about **4 MB**, reduce it to about 3 MB (like you did for the Bruce folder). Anything 2000 px or wider is perfect.
4. Put them all in a Drive folder called **Full Quality**. Keep the original file names if you can. If not, don't worry: I match them by date and by what's in the picture.

You don't need all of them. The ones marked ★ are the episode covers and matter most.
